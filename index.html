Quick Reference · How to Add Works
Adding a new work to this page
  • Go to your GitHub repository and open works.html
  • Click the pencil/edit icon ✏️
  • Find the section (Articles, Maps, or Reports)
  • Delete the empty-state block in that section
  • Copy the template in the comments and fill in your details
  • Click Commit changes — your site updates instantly
TIP: To add more cards, copy the entire <div class="work-card">...</div> block and paste it inside the works-grid div. Change the title, description, year and link.
