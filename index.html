Nigeria is not a problem to be solved. It is a landscape of stories, systems, and people — waiting for the right frameworks to be understood.

I sit at the intersection of place, policy, and people. I write with data. I think with maps. I speak for communities that research too often talks about, not with.

My work bridges academic insight and public understanding — making geography matter beyond the classroom.
